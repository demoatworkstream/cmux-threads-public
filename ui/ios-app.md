# UI: the Threads iOS app

Read this when the user asks about the phone app, or a send from it reaches you.

The Threads app (repo `~/Projects/threads-mobile`, not included here) is a client of threadsd and nothing else. Its contract:

- **One API, one schema.** It renders only from `GET /state` and `GET /events` (schema_version 1) and acts only through `POST /send`, `POST /prompt/answer` and `POST /interrupt`. See [threadsd/API.md](../threadsd/API.md). It never reads screens or talks to cmux.
- **Capabilities, not agent names.** Thread cells, question cards and composers key their controls on `session.capabilities` (`submit`, `interrupt`, `remote_control`, `attachments`, `usage_limit_retry`, `prompts`). `agent` / `agent_label` is only a badge (Claude, Codex). A new agent type needs no app change.
- **Screens:** Needs you (each `needs_you` thread, its numbered `questions`, one answer box per question), Projects (roots, then threads with marks, all machines), Chat with Master (`/sessions/master/messages` plus `message` events), Artifacts, live working indicators from `state`, and photo/file attachments.
- **Routing:** an answer to a question goes to that thread with `question_id` (`kind` "answer"), so threadsd prefixes `Answer to question <n> ("<text>"):` and a short "Yes" stays attached to its question. Anything else goes to `target: "master"`.
- **Open dialogs (`session.prompt`):** an AskUserQuestion, permission or confirm dialog hides the session's input box. The app shows it as a choice card in that chat (Master, thread page, Needs you): radio rows for single choice (a tap answers it), checkboxes plus Send for multi-select, and "Other…" for free text when `allows_other`. It answers through `POST /prompt/answer` (one entry per question; threadsd presses the keys and checks the dialog closed). Hidden when `capabilities.prompts` is false.
- **Quick-select answers:** a question with `options` (from "(pick one)" / "(pick any)" or a numbered list under it) is ONE card: the question, then its options. Short options are chips, long ones a row each. *Pick one*: a tap picks and sends after 2.5 s, with Undo. *Pick any*: tap several, then "Send 1, 3". "Other…" (unless `allow_other` is false) adds typed text, which sends from the field itself. The reply is plain text the session reads: "2", "1, 3", "1, 3 — plus: <text>", or the text alone, sent as `/send` kind "answer" with `question_id`. The sent answer reads back as the option names. `parse: "heuristic"` adds a quiet "options read from the message". No `options`: the usual answer box (Yes/No for yes/no questions). Skip stays on every card.
- **Queued sends:** while a dialog is open, `/send` answers `queued` ("answer the question above first"). The app shows "Queued · …" under the message, flips it to Delivered from the `send` event when threadsd delivers it after the dialog closes, and offers "Send as the answer above" to use the typed text as the Other answer instead. With a dialog open, Send also hides the keyboard and scrolls to the question.
- **Working indicator:** a typing bubble after a send until the reply lands (from live `state`, plus the just-sent message while no reply has come yet).
- **No housekeeping rows:** `system: true` rows are hidden from the Master chat.
- **Question time:** each question (and dialog) shows "asked 25m ago · 2:16 AM" (with the date when not today) from `asked_at`; tapping it opens that session's conversation at `message_id`, highlighted. Questions marked `stale` (or no longer listed) are dimmed with "The thread has moved on since" but stay answerable. Needs you lists fresh waits first, then moved-on ones, each oldest-asked first. `last_status` shows as one secondary "Latest:" line. A long last report ends at a word with "…" and a More that opens the whole message.
- **Skip = clear.** One control: Skip on a question, Skip all on a thread card (or Skip on a card with no numbered question). It only takes the item off Needs you on every device (`/send` kind `skip` / `skip_all`, stored in threadsd); **nothing is ever sent to the session**. Undo appears right after; a Skipped list at the bottom of Needs you has Restore (`unskip`). The thread page keeps skipped questions in its history. A skipped item comes back when the thread asks a new question.
- **Voice brain (Settings):** a list, not a text field. *Automatic* (default) uses the first brain `GET /voice/brains` reports `up`, else Apple's on-device model; then *On this iPhone*; then each listed brain with a live dot and latency (refreshed every 10 s while Settings is open); then *Custom address…*, which shows the address field. The footer and the call screen (under the "Threads" pill) show which brain a call uses. A call resolves the choice when it starts; a chosen brain that's down falls back to the iPhone and says so, and if it stops answering mid-call the call switches to the iPhone once, says so, and stays there.
- **Crash and error reports:** the app keeps its last 200 events (call phases, mic taps, brain requests). A crash (Swift trap, Objective-C exception, signal) writes them and the stack to a file as it dies; at the next launch that, Apple's MetricKit report, and any non-fatal errors go to `POST /ui/diagnostics`, stored on the Mac in `~/.local/share/threadsd/diagnostics/`.
- **Artifacts page:** the fourth page in the pager. It lists `GET /artifacts` (Artifact Shelf's index on the Mac, served by threadsd) as cards like the Claude app's Artifacts list: the type icon, title, "made in <thread>" (`thread.name`, else `workspace.title`, else `session_title`), "Edited N hr. ago", and a pin. Pinned artifacts come first, then Today / Yesterday / This week / Earlier. The filter menu groups by project or thread, or shows pinned only. Search sits at the bottom. A long press gives Pin, Go to the thread, Copy Link and Share. Pins go through `POST /artifacts/{id}/pin`, so they sync with the Mac Shelf, and the page refetches on the `artifact` event. A tap opens the artifact in-app, in a web view signed in to its `account`. Each account has its own persistent cookie store, so you sign in once per account with **Continue with email**, because Google blocks in-app sign-in. When claude.ai says "Page not found", the account is probably the wrong one: the banner offers Switch, and the app remembers the choice for that artifact. The ⋯ menu has Open in Claude App as a fallback.
- **Ending a call:** one owner (CallCenter) and one state machine (idle → starting → live → ended). The end button, the call screen closing, and iOS hanging up (lock screen, Dynamic Island, car, headset) all end it the same way, once: CallKit `CXEndCallAction` (waited for; if CallKit refuses or stays silent, the call is reported ended anyway), speech and mic and barge-in monitor stopped, `AVAudioSession` deactivated, call screen closed. Nothing restarts after that, even a hang-up during the greeting. Going to the home screen or locking the phone keeps the call as a real CallKit call: the green pill or Dynamic Island brings it back. Each hang-up is a `voice.hangup` span on the call trace (`callkit.end`: fulfilled / ended by iOS / reported ended (…), `audio.engine_running`, `audio.session`). One `CXProvider` for the app, never one per call.
- **Call traces and Call history:** every call is one OpenTelemetry trace sent to threadsd (`POST /v1/traces`, OTLP JSON): listening, speech to text, the brain's decision and each model request, tool runs, sends with their result, spoken replies, barge-ins, time to first spoken word, errors, and **what the screen showed** (each confirm card word for word, its buttons, and whether it was tapped, answered by voice or replaced). Spans are kept on disk as they end, so a call cut off by a crash still arrives at the next launch, and the crash report carries the call's `trace_id`. Settings → Call history lists `GET /voice/calls`; a call opens its timeline and transcript (`GET /voice/calls/<id>`), with thumbs up/down and a note (`POST …/rating`). The "Call ended" row in the Master chat opens the same page. Rated calls become the brain's training examples (good) and regression cases (bad).
- **What reaches you:** sends arrive in your input box like typed text, logged in `~/.local/share/threadsd/sends.jsonl` with client `Threads/…`. Attachments arrive as `Attached: <absolute path>` lines. Treat them as the user's words.
